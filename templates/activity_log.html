Coverage, retention & storage

This history starts when the update is deployed. It cannot reconstruct earlier changes. Database changes are recorded together with the change, including bulk operations. Changes made directly in Google Drive or outside the ERP are not tracked here.

Edits store changed fields only. Created and deleted records keep a text snapshot, without passwords or authentication secrets. Files are not copied into this log. Related rows from one operation share a reference in their details.

Entries older than 90 days are automatically removed in batches while the ERP is in use. There is no permanent archive or Undo in this version. A database owner can bypass database protections; ERP administrators cannot.
